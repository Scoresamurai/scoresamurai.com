Is 1100 a good SAT score?

A 1100 is above most test takers. It sits under the middle 50 percent at every one of the ten schools below.

Good is whatever gets you into the schools on your list. So the useful questions are where a 1100 stands among test takers and at the schools you want.

A 1100 is higher than 67 percent of all US 11th and 12th graders. Among students who actually took the SAT, it is higher than 62 percent.

Source: College Board, Understanding SAT Scores, SAT percentile ranks, research.collegeboard.org, read October 12 2026.

The first figure is the nationally representative percentile, a weighted sample of all US 11th and 12th graders. The second is the SAT user percentile, from students in the past three graduating classes who took the test.

The submit rule

  1. Where the school requires a score, you send it, so the work is to raise it before the Done Date.
  2. Where the school is test-optional, send a score inside or above its middle 50 percent. Hold one that sits under.
  3. Where the school is test-free, the score is not read, so it changes nothing there.
