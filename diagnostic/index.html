Where the points went

Math: 19 questions missed.

  • The Half-Finished Move: 5
  • The Region Has to Be Checked, Not Eyeballed: 2
  • Foundations (White belt moves and things not yet learned): 8
  • Other: 4

Reading and Writing: 20 questions missed.

  • The Answer to a Different Question: 5
  • Can Each Half Stand Alone: 4
  • Foundations (White belt moves): 10
  • Other: 1

What it means: Three Green moves cost Maya the most, and they lead the plan. Foundations holds the White belt moves and the things not yet learned, worked in alongside the Green work. Each move has a rule, and a rule carries over to every test after this one.

The three moves costing the most

  1. The Half-Finished Move (Math, 5 misses). The right method, stopped one step early. The wrong answer is the student's own unfinished work, which is why it looks right.
  2. The Answer to a Different Question (Reading and Writing, 5 misses). Maya reads the passage, then picks something true that the question never asked. It is the most common Reading and Writing move at this score.
  3. Can Each Half Stand Alone (Reading and Writing, 4 misses). Two full sentences joined by a comma alone. Each half could stand on its own, so the comma can't hold them.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What does it cost?

The plan sets the number of sessions, and the free Diagnostic sets the plan. The price comes in writing with the Diagnostic, before you decide anything.

Do you guarantee a score?

No. Guarantees in test prep come with pages of conditions, and a score depends on the student doing the work between sessions. What is promised is this: a written Diagnostic before you pay, the same teacher in every session, and a written update every week so you can see for yourself whether the traps are closing.

Is there a call?

Only if you want one. You can book a free 15-minute Zoom with Sensei Jay, a parent on, when you submit the form or after the written Diagnostic arrives. He walks through the Diagnostic and the plan. No pitch deck. It is optional; the written Diagnostic has everything a family needs to decide.

What happens to our information?

It is used to prepare the Diagnostic, and for the weekly email only if you ask for it. No resale. Privacy
