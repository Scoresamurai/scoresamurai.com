The un-school test

Why the SAT matters more now.

Colleges including MIT, Dartmouth, Harvard and Stanford have brought test requirements back, saying scores help predict how students do there.

MIT, March 28 2022, Dean Stu Schmill. Dartmouth, February 5 2024, President Sian Leah Beilock, citing a study by Dartmouth faculty. Harvard, April 11 2024, Dean Hopi Hoekstra. Stanford, June 7 2024, its faculty admissions committee.

The way Sensei Jay reads it, that is only half the story: AI now sits inside schoolwork, grades have drifted upward, and a grade means something different in every school. The SAT is the one measure every applicant takes under the same conditions.

Pew Research Center, February 24 2026: 54 percent of US teens have used AI chatbots for schoolwork (survey September 25 to October 9 2025). ACT, May 16 2022: the average high school GPA rose from 3.17 in 2010 to 3.36 in 2021.

Your transcript says what your school says. The score says what you did on the day.

The method

The Digital SAT is a pattern recognition game, not purely an intelligence test.

Built from 1,168 real SAT questions, catalogued one at a time.

The Dojo Roadmap is the plan. It is built from the student's own misses, not from a syllabus. It lists the moves to close in the order they cost points at that score, and it is rewritten as belts are earned. No two Roadmaps are the same.

The same moves, every test. The wrong answers are built from the same handful of habits, year after year. A procedure stopped one step early. A first meaning trusted too fast. A true sentence that answers a question nobody asked. Once a student can name the move, it stops working.

The Desmos work. Desmos is built into the Math section. Half of the timing problem is knowing which questions to send to it. The rest is knowing which to work by hand. We decide that question by question, until the choice is a reflex.

Weekly tracking. Speed and accuracy are tracked every week, by section, and reported to the parent in the written note. Nobody has to ask how it is going.

Why students get stuck. No two students are stuck for the same reason. One loses points to the clock. Another picks the answer that is true and still wrong. A third knows the math cold and freezes when a question looks unfamiliar.

You already know the material. You are losing points to habits that have names.

The way Sensei Jay sees it, AI can answer the question. It cannot see why this student keeps missing it. That is the difference between an app and a Sensei.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Anything not here, ask on the Diagnostic form. We answer every one.

How long does SAT prep take?

It depends on the size of the gap and the runway before the test date. That is what the Diagnostic measures. Starting late turns the work into a sprint. Starting far too early turns it into drift.

The honest starting point is where the Diagnostic says the student is, not where the calendar says they should be.

What is a good SAT score for selective schools?

At the most selective schools, the middle of the admitted class now sits at 1500 and above. One tier down, 1350 to 1450 does the work.

The number that matters is where the admitted range starts at the school the student wants. That is the number the Roadmap is built to reach. Each school publishes its range in its Common Data Set, and the Diagnostic reads the list against it.

Do colleges require the SAT?

As of September 2026: Harvard, Stanford, Georgetown and the University of Miami require an SAT or ACT score from first-year applicants. Princeton and Notre Dame bring the requirement back for students applying in the 2027 to 2028 cycle.

Duke, Michigan, USC, Boston College, Wisconsin, Penn State, Oregon and Rutgers are test-optional. The University of California campuses do not look at scores. Check each school's page before applying, since policies change every cycle.
