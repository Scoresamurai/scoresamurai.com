How sure is this?

College Board publishes no conversion table. It scores each question by its difficulty, so the same number right can earn different scores.

That is why the Lab gives a range, never one number. Even on the real test, College Board says a score can move about 40 points on a retake.

On the easier Module 2, practice-test analyses put the section top in the high 500s to low 600s.

The table behind the Lab is our estimate, built from published examples. Real scores will tune it. College Board publishes no Module 1 cutoff either, so near the line the range covers both routes.
